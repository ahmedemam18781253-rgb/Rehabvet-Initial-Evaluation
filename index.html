Objective Findings

Orthopedic lameness (posture / stance)

0 = normal stance / no lameness

1 = slightly abnormal stance (PWB)

2 = moderately abnormal stance (PWB-TTWB)

3 = severely abnormal stance (TTWB-NWB)

4 = unable / unwilling to bear weight (NWB)

Orthopedic lameness (at walk & trot)

0 = normal walk/trot, no lameness

1 = slight or intermittent lameness

2 = obvious weight bearing lameness

3 = severe weight bearing lameness

4 = intermittent non-weight bearing lameness

5 = continuous non-weight bearing lameness

Neurologic grading scheme

5 = normal strength / coordination

4 = can stand to support; minimal paresis and ataxia

3 = can stand to support but frequently stumbles and falls; mild paraparesis and ataxia

2 = unable to stand or support; moves limbs readily with assistance; moderate ataxia and paresis

1 = unable to stand or support; slight movement when supported by the tail; severe paresis

0 = absence of purposeful movement; tetra- or paraplegia
